Marcus Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

In short: Marcus keeps everything you enter on your iPhone. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code, and the developer receives no data from the app.

What Marcus stores, and where

Marcus stores the following only on your iPhone:

  • Goals: the name, colour and icon you choose, whether a goal counts time or sessions, how often it repeats, its target and deadline, and whether it appears on Home.
  • Sessions: when each one started and ended, how long it lasted or how many it counted, the note you write, if any, and where it happened, if you let Marcus record places.
  • For the widget and the lock screen: a copy of each goal's name, colour, icon and progress, and the timer that is running, kept in storage on the same iPhone that only Marcus and its widget can read.
  • One setting: whether Marcus records places.

Marcus never sends this information anywhere. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, the app's data is included in that backup, as it is for other apps, under Apple's backup protections.

Location

Marcus uses your location only if you allow it, only while you are using the app, and never in the background.

  • When you start a timer or log a session in the app, Marcus asks iOS for one location fix and saves it with that session, on your iPhone, so it can show your goals on a map.
  • To give a place a readable name, such as a street and city, Marcus sends the place's coordinates to Apple's map service. When you search for a place by name, your search text and the area the map shows are sent to Apple Maps. Apple handles these requests under the Apple Privacy Policy; the developer of Marcus does not receive them.
  • If you turn off Precise Location for Marcus, iOS gives Marcus only an approximate position, and Marcus names the place by its city alone.
  • You can stop recording places at any time with the Record places switch on the map in Marcus's History tab, or in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security, Location Services, Marcus.

Information the developer receives

None from the app. If you email support, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. The draft Marcus prepares includes the app's version and your iOS version, so we can reproduce a problem. We use your email only to reply to you and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Children

Marcus collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete any session from History, and any goal from the Goals tab, at any time.
  • Deleting Marcus from your iPhone deletes all of its data, including the widget's copy. Data in earlier device backups is removed when you delete those backups.

Changes to this policy

If Marcus ever handles your information differently, this page will be updated before that version of the app is released, and the date above will change.

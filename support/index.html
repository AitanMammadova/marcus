The quickest way to write is from Marcus itself: tap the ⓘ button on Home, then Contact Support. The email it prepares already names your app and iOS versions.

Getting started

  • Add a goal on the Goals tab. Choose whether it counts time or sessions, and whether it repeats daily, weekly or monthly or has one total target.
  • Log time on the Log tab: tap a goal to start its timer, then Stop & Save. A goal that counts sessions logs +1 with each tap.
  • Log a past session by swiping left on a timed goal on the Log tab, or by touching and holding it.
  • See your progress on Home, and every session, grouped by day, in History. Tap a goal to see its last six months.

Widget and lock screen

  • Add the Marcus widget from your Home Screen's edit mode. It can start and stop a timer and log +1 without opening the app.
  • While a timer runs, the lock screen and the Dynamic Island show it. If they do not, check that Live Activities are on in Settings > Marcus.
  • A session ended from the widget or the lock screen reaches History the next time you open Marcus.

Places

  • Marcus can note where each session starts and show your goals on a map in History. It asks iOS for permission the first time you start a timer.
  • To change your answer later, use the Record places switch on that map, or Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Marcus.
  • You can also give any session a place by hand: tap it in History and choose Set place.

Common questions

Why wasn't my session saved?

Sessions shorter than five minutes are not saved. Marcus asks before it throws a short timer away, so you can keep it running instead.

My timer stopped by itself.

A timer that runs for 12 hours is stopped, because it was almost certainly forgotten. Marcus then asks whether to keep the first 12 hours.

The widget doesn't show my latest session.

Open Marcus once. The widget updates whenever you leave the app.

Marcus says it can't open my data.

Choose Prepare a copy of the data file, then Email support, and send us the copy before you reset anything.

Your data

Everything stays on your iPhone. Marcus has no account and collects no data; see the Privacy Policy. To delete everything, delete the app.
